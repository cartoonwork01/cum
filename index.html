Billing & Payments Management System (BPMS)

Every bill has a unique reference number called the “Payment Code”. With this payment code, payments can be made through the approved Channels.

About BPMS:
The BPMS is a centralized repository for authentic bills and demand notices from all Ministries, Departments and Agencies of the Ogun State Government. All genuine bills from the OGSG BPMS have a unique Payment Code as well as a QR Code that can be used to validate authenticity of all bills. The BPMS also enables online and bank branch payment for all MDA bills. A receipt of payment is also automatically generated and sent to payer as well as the Ogun State MDA.
